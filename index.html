About Me

I'm Daniel A. Dare, founder of Horizon Literary Group, a book marketing agency based in the UK. I help fiction and non-fiction authors reach more of the right readers through targeted ad campaigns, community outreach, and long-term visibility strategies.

My approach is simple: I identify the specific reader communities most likely to love your book, then build a targeted plan to get your book in front of them. Whether you're a debut author or have a backlist of titles, I can help you find the readers who are already looking for books like yours.

I work with authors across genres—from romance and fantasy to business and Christian non-fiction. Every strategy is built around your book, your goals, and your readers. No templates. No generic advice. Just a plan that makes sense for you.

What I Do

Targeted strategies to get your book in front of the readers who will love it.

🚀

Pre-Launch Strategy

Mapping out a timeline months in advance, coordinating cover reveals, organizing street teams, and setting up pre-order campaigns to build initial momentum before launch day. I help you plan every step so your book launches with maximum visibility and early sales momentum.

📰

PR & Media Outreach

Pitching your book to journalists, bloggers, podcasters, and literary reviewers to secure interviews, features, articles, and media coverage. I build targeted media lists, craft compelling pitches, and handle follow-ups so you can focus on writing your next book.

💬

Platform & Community Building

Building and nurturing your author platform across social media, newsletters, and reader communities. I help you grow an engaged audience that shows up for every launch—turning casual readers into loyal fans who recommend your books to others.

📊

Paid Advertising Management

Running targeted Amazon Ads, Facebook Ads, and BookBub campaigns that reach readers already searching for books like yours. I optimize for conversions, not just clicks—so every dollar you spend works harder for you.

🎨

Book Banners & Trailers

Designing eye-catching book banners, social media graphics, and cinematic book trailers that capture the tone and emotion of your story. Perfect for launch campaigns, social media promotion, and your author website.

💻

Website Design

Building clean, professional author websites that convert visitors into readers. From single-page landing sites to full multi-page author platforms, I create sites that showcase your books, capture emails, and reflect your author brand.

🔍

Metadata & Amazon Optimization

Helping your book rank higher in search results and online bookstore listings by choosing the right keywords, categories, and A+ Content. This is the foundation of all book marketing—if readers can't find you, they can't buy you.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the scope of the project and the platforms involved. I offer flexible packages for debut authors and established authors alike. Get in touch and we'll find something that works for you.

How long until I see results?

Most authors see meaningful results within 30–60 days. Ad campaigns typically take 1–2 weeks to optimize, and community outreach builds momentum over time.

Do you work with all genres?

I specialize in fiction and non-fiction, including romance, fantasy, thrillers, Christian books, children's books, poetry, and business books. If you're not sure whether your book is a fit, send me a message and I'll let you know honestly.

What do you need from me?

Just your book details, your goals, and access to your ad accounts if we're running campaigns. I handle the rest.
