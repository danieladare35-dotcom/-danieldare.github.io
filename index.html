Questions Authors Ask

A straightforward way to start.

Do you work with different types of books?

Yes. The approach is adapted to the subject, genre, audience, and goals of each individual book.

What does reader discovery mean?

It means identifying the readers and communities most likely to value a book and finding practical ways to put the book in front of them.

Do you guarantee sales?

No. Promotion can improve exposure and create opportunities for discovery, but no ethical marketer can guarantee a particular number of sales, reviews, or readers.

Can I see examples of your previous work?

Yes. Relevant examples and results can be shared when discussing a project, including work that is appropriate to show privately.
